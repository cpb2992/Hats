# Hats privacy policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Hats is a planner for teachers and anyone who wears a lot of hats. It was built to keep your information on your phone.

## What Hats collects

**Nothing is sent to us.** Hats has no accounts, no servers, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. We never see your tasks, calendar, students or settings.

Everything you put in Hats (tasks, events, your bell schedule, your weekly rhythm, Caseload goals and data points, Pulse sorts, the kids' board) is saved only in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling Hats or tapping **Settings → Erase everything** deletes it.

## Student information

Hats is designed so you never need to enter a student's name. Caseload uses initials (like "J.S."), a class section and plan type. Please don't enter full names, ID numbers or other details that identify a student. That information stays on your phone.

## Microphone and speech

When you tap **Say anything** and talk, Hats uses your phone's built-in speech recognition (on most Android phones, Google's speech service) to turn your words into text. Hats only listens while you're holding a capture open. How your phone's speech service handles audio is covered by that service's own policy (for Google, see https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can always type instead.

## Optional: Claude

If you choose to add your own Claude API key in **Settings → Say anything**, then what you say or type in Say anything is sent directly from your phone to Anthropic, together with a short summary of your day (your schedule, open tasks, upcoming events and student initials) so it can understand you. Hats sends this only when you use Say anything with a key set. Anthropic's handling is covered by its own policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Without a key, Say anything works entirely on your phone. Your key is stored in your phone's secure storage.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification content passes through a server.

## Ratings

Hats may show Google Play's built-in rating prompt after you've used it for a while. Google handles that prompt; Hats doesn't receive your rating.

## Children

Hats is made for adults (teachers and parents). It isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't collect information from them. The kids' bedtime board is used by a parent on the parent's own phone or tablet.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
