# Hats privacy policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

Hats is a planner for people who wear a lot of hats: family, home, work, friends and yourself. It's built to keep your life on your phone, and to send things elsewhere only when you turn that on.

## On your phone (always)

Everything you put in Hats is saved on your phone, encrypted: your hats, to-dos, packs (1:1s, invoices, deadlines, care reminders and the rest), calendar, meals and groceries, people and pets, notes, reflections, spending targets and streaks. Photos you attach stay on the phone. Hats has no ads and no analytics or tracking.

## Optional: an account and sync

If you sign in (with your email), Hats keeps a copy of your data on Hats' sync server so you can restore it or use it on another device. It travels over an encrypted connection and each account can only read its own data, but it is **not end-to-end encrypted**: it's stored by our database provider (Supabase) and protected by their security. You can stop syncing and sign out at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and synced data (see Contact).

## Optional: family sharing

If you start or join a family in Settings → Family sharing, the things you choose to share (groceries, the kitchen list, the dinner plan, date nights that aren't surprises, family-calendar events and to-dos you mark as shared) are stored on the sync server so everyone in the family can see and change them. Everything else stays private to you. Leaving the family keeps what's already on your phone.

## Optional: connected services

- **Google Calendar** (read-only). If you connect it, Hats reads events from the calendars you pick for the next 30 days. It never creates, changes or deletes your Google events. Google's use is covered by its own policy.
- **WHOOP.** If you connect it, Hats reads your recovery and sleep to plan lighter days. You can disconnect it in Settings.
- **Speech.** When you talk to Hats, your phone's built-in speech recognition (on most Android phones, Google's speech service) turns your words into text. Hats only listens while you're capturing. You can always type instead.

Hats doesn't sell your data or use it for advertising, and it doesn't share it with anyone except the services above when you turn them on.

## Information about other people

Hats lets you keep names and short notes about your family, friends, pets and the people in your packs. Keep it to what helps you plan. It stays on your phone unless you sync or share it as described above.

## Ratings

Hats may show Google Play's built-in rating prompt after you've used it for a while. Google handles that prompt; Hats doesn't receive your rating.

## Children

Hats is made for adults. It isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them. Kids' routines and activities are entered by a parent on the parent's phone.

## Deleting your data

- On the phone: uninstall Hats, or use Settings to erase it.
- Synced and shared data: sign in and delete your account in Settings, or email us and we'll delete it.
